Welcome to Bear Bites Deli Order Page

Bear Bites Deli offers built to order subs with a variety of fresh ingredients. Since 2021, Bear Bites Deli has gained vast popularity amongst students. To help solve this, the Computer Science club has created this site to pre-order your sub.

Order your sub online then pick up your sub during your requested lunch period at Bear Bites Deli. You will still need to stop at the cashier to pay for your sub at the time of pick-up. (Orders MUST be placed by 9:00 a.m. daily to be ready for lunch).

**A limited number of pre-orders will be accepted daily and prioritized on a first come first serve basis
